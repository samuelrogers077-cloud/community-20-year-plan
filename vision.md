# My 20-Year Vision for My Community 🌍

## The Core Problem
Many students in my community do not understand Artificial Intelligence (AI) or how it can improve their lives. 

## The Solution
I want to create a program that teaches students practical AI skills, showing them how to use technology to elevate their lifestyle and solve problems in our society.

## The 20-Year Goal
*   **Years 1-5:** Learn AI and build a pilot program for local students. Find a partner school.
*   **Years 6-10:** Expand the program to multiple schools in the county.
*   **Years 11-20:** Build a community tech hub where students can use AI to solve local environmental and agricultural problems.
